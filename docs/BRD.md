# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## QR-Based Billing System

### Executive Summary
A comprehensive billing solution for retail stores that uses QR codes for product management, enabling fast and secure product scanning, inventory tracking, and sales analytics.

### Business Objectives
- Streamline retail billing process with QR technology
- Provide real-time inventory and sales tracking
- Ensure secure product data management
- Enable profit analysis and business insights
- Support multiple sharing and printing options

### Target Users
- Retail store owners and managers
- Cashier/counter staff
- Inventory managers
- Business analysts

### Key Business Features

#### 1. Product Management
- **Add Products**: Create product entries with pricing, tax, and inventory details
- **Product Catalog**: Maintain digital product database with QR codes
- **QR Code Generation**: Automatic QR code creation for each product
- **Product Display**: View products with QR/barcode visualization

#### 2. Billing Operations
- **QR Scanning**: Continuous product scanning for fast checkout
- **Cart Management**: Add, remove, modify quantities in real-time
- **Discount Management**: Apply product-level and bill-level discounts
- **Tax Calculation**: Automatic tax computation per product
- **Customer Information**: Capture customer details for receipts

#### 3. Sales Analytics
- **Daily Sales Tracking**: Monitor daily revenue and profit
- **Monthly Reports**: View monthly sales, profit, and cost analysis
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify sales patterns and performance indicators
- **Profit Calculations**: Real-time profit/loss analysis

#### 4. Communication & Sharing
- **WhatsApp Sharing**: Send bills directly to customers via WhatsApp
- **Email Integration**: Email bill receipts to customers
- **PDF Generation**: Professional bill formatting
- **Thermal Printing**: Hardware receipt printing support

#### 5. Security & Validation
- **Store Security**: Unique store identifiers prevent cross-store QR usage
- **Data Encryption**: Sensitive pricing data protected
- **Validation Rules**: Automatic loss prevention alerts
- **PIN Protection**: Secure settings access

### Business Rules

#### Product Management Rules
- Each product must have unique QR code
- Selling price cannot be below purchase price (with warnings)
- Tax calculation based on discounted selling price
- Original price tracking for MRP reference

#### Billing Rules
- Multiple products can be scanned continuously
- Duplicate scans increase quantity (not add new items)
- Product discounts reduce taxable amount
- Bill discounts applied after all product calculations
- Loss validation: Highlight when selling < purchase costs

#### Sales Tracking Rules
- Daily aggregation by date
- Monthly filtering with year selection
- Profit calculation: (Sales - COGS) - Discounts
- Trend indicators: Up/down arrows vs previous day

#### Security Rules
- QR codes only valid for originating store
- Sensitive data (purchase prices) hidden by default
- Store secret changes invalidate existing QR codes
- PIN required for settings access

### User Journey

#### Store Setup
1. Install app and set PIN
2. Configure store secret
3. Add products to catalog
4. Generate QR codes for products

#### Daily Operations
1. Start billing session
2. Scan products continuously
3. Adjust quantities and apply discounts
4. Review totals and validation warnings
5. Process payment and share/print receipt

#### Business Analysis
1. View daily sales performance
2. Analyze monthly trends
3. Track profit margins
4. Identify top-selling products

### Success Metrics
- Reduced checkout time (target: 50% faster)
- Improved inventory accuracy
- Real-time profit visibility
- Customer satisfaction with digital receipts
- Reduced billing errors

### Constraints & Assumptions
- Android/iOS mobile devices required
- Internet connection for initial setup only
- Thermal printer optional (software printing available)
- QR codes generated by app only
- Single store per app installation
